# VNIBB recovery and improvement plan

Updated 28 October 2026. Goal: restore useful, traceable financial statements first, then ship the existing workflow repairs and recover sustainable market coverage. Planning only; no production mutation, deployment or issue closure is authorized by this document.

## Evidence and decisions

Read-only comparison found matching FPT/VCI FY2025 annual income, balance-sheet and cash-flow values on production `cloud-01` and `deploy-01`; production has more statement rows. Both sampled legacy rows lack `_financial_lineage`. Replaying the deployed SQL fallback retained their periods but withheld monetary values for missing currency/unit/reporting-basis evidence. This proves that fallback path, not every live response. SSI corroborates sampled revenue/gross profit. Selected FPT original income, balance and cash-flow figures have been inspected directly; VCI originals and other FPT fields remain unverified by these checks.

**FPT original-page check:** `deploy-01` resource `financial_report_sources/FPT/Annual/2025/FPT_Baocaotaichinh_2025_Kiemtoan_Hopnhat.pdf`, SHA-256 `630f61f6ef9f07d5c593c3bf8f65bad1d56ecbb091921296ed5c4e830ea070a4`, matches its catalog hash. PDF pages 1–2 identify the FY2025 consolidated statements and the 19 March 2026 disclosure. PDF page 12 (printed page 9), form B02-DN/HN, explicitly labels the 2025 column VND: code 10 net revenue 70,112,825,100,710; code 20 gross profit 25,888,529,512,413; code 60 total after-tax profit 11,232,339,450,734; code 61 parent-shareholder profit 9,376,127,629,501. The legacy `net_income` matches code 61, not code 60; do not relabel it as total profit. Codes 22 finance costs and 23 interest are distinct. This is direct inspection of the retained PDF, not signature validation, a fresh issuer download, or whole-report certification.

**FPT balance and cash-flow spot checks:** Original consolidated balance-sheet PDF pages 8–9 (printed 5–6) label 31 December 2025 balances VND: cash and cash equivalents 10,522,105,729,992; total assets 88,141,991,634,625. Consolidated indirect cash-flow PDF pages 13–14 (printed 10–11) label the year ended 31 December 2025 VND: operating cash flow 10,136,043,915,911; investing cash flow −11,624,743,591,629; financing cash flow 2,801,322,869,532; ending cash 10,522,105,729,992. These are selected fields, not a complete statement or source-use authorization; inspect every proposed field before recovery.

**28 October stored-row sample:** a read-only PostgreSQL inventory of FPT, VCI, VNM and MSR found 24 statement/symbol/period groups. None of the sampled balance-sheet or cash-flow rows had `_financial_lineage`; some income rows did. FPT and MSR annual balance sheets each contain 24 rows with fiscal years outside 1900–2100. These are stored-row counts, not admitted coverage: a full field-level admission matrix and explicit expected-period denominator remain outstanding.

Keep the current stores; do not copy a database or redesign the stack. Recover supported fields using existing admission and natural-key upserts. Do not infer units by magnitude, certify a whole report from two matching figures, relabel issuer evidence as Vietcap, or disable validation. Financial recovery does not depend on whole-market Universe recovery; separately verify source-use rights for the intended serving operation.

## Ordered work and acceptance

- [ ] **1. Establish the financial coverage baseline.** Record serving image/frontend identities and inventory each statement type by symbol, annual/quarter period, source, populated key fields, retained evidence and admission reason. Separate missing rows, rejected fields, source failures and consumer faults; use retained-store reads rather than acquisition-capable GETs. **Accept:** a reproducible stored-versus-admitted matrix for income, balance and cash flow, including FPT/VCI FY2025 and the VNM/MSR QA cases, with explicit denominators.
- [ ] **2. Verify recoverable originals; start with FPT FY2025.** Inspect the retained audited consolidated PDF directly and retain document hash, page/table, fiscal/revision identity, currency, scale, field mapping and conversion. Verify income, balance and cash flow; distinguish parent profit from total profit, interest from finance costs, EPS from amount units, and consolidated from parent-only reports. Seek equivalent authorized VCI evidence; SSI is corroboration, not automatic provenance for every field. **Accept:** reviewed field evidence for every amount proposed for recovery; unsupported fields remain identified, not guessed.
- [ ] **3. Repair the ingestion-to-serving contract (after 2).** Reuse `financial_service.py` admission, the structured writers in `data_pipeline.py`/`cache_manager.py`, SQL fallback in `equity.py`, and MCP admission. Retain the original legacy record separately from a newly evidenced canonical observation; preserve source ownership and unrelated periods/fields. Explicit table/report unit declarations may support mapped monetary fields within their verified scope; per-share and ratio units remain separate. Ensure authorized recovered-source identities survive API/MCP source selection rather than fabricating an IQ envelope. Trace and fence bypass writers before production recovery: `apps/api/scripts/v46_data_repair.py:551-605` fills null EPS without lineage and permits arbitrary same-year fallback; no caller or execution receipt was found, so it is not established as the incident cause. Do not use it for recovery; retire its unsafe write path or route it through exact fiscal/source/basis admission with concurrency-safe persistence. **Accept:** disposable replay persists and serves the exact verified numbers through SQL, API and MCP; conflicting evidence is withheld, genuine zero/negative values survive, unsupported fields stay null with specific reasons, and legacy repair cannot fill rejected EPS, substitute another quarter, or overwrite recovered lineage during a concurrent write.

**Step 3 reader cutover gate:** after disposable verification and separate rollout approval, deploy the compatible API/MCP financial readers and unsafe-writer fences before changing production financial rows. Update every applicable source-selection path, including MCP collection queries and latest/snapshot reads; admit only explicitly supported, evidence-validated recovered sources, without relabeling issuer observations as `vietcap`/`VCI`. Bind the serving API/MCP to tested immutable artifacts, verify existing-source reads and guarded rejection behavior, and retain rollback. Keep old reader artifacts for pre-backfill rollback only; after backfill, any rollback must preserve recovered-source compatibility or coordinate the proven data reversal.
- [ ] **4. Recover production financials in bounded batches (after 1–3, verified production reader cutover and operational approval).** Before any row mutation, confirm the serving API/MCP identities satisfy step 3's reader cutover gate. Take a fresh paired backup, prove off-source restore, preview an exact row/field diff, and apply the smallest verified FPT annual batch first. Use existing natural keys and transaction safety; retain raw evidence and document a reversible data change. Inspect stored columns and canonical lineage, not only writer counts. Invalidate only affected financial caches after commit. **Accept:** actual FPT FY2025 income, balance and cash-flow widgets/API/MCP agree with reviewed originals; replay changes nothing, unrelated data is unchanged, and compatible application rollback or coordinated data reversal is proven before expansion.
- [ ] **5. Expand usable statement coverage and prevent recurrence (after 4).** Prioritize VCI, VNM, MSR and the user's watchlist, then other supported symbols/periods against the baseline denominator. Preserve units, reporting basis and revision evidence on every new scheduled write. Quarterly flows need known single-quarter/YTD treatment; balance-sheet TTM uses the latest compatible snapshot, while flow TTM needs four compatible quarters. Make missing-unit and failed-ingestion outcomes actionable through existing job records. **Accept:** source-backed annual/quarter/TTM boundary cases and the following scheduled financial update pass; publish recovered/withheld/missing coverage, without claiming all-market completion.

## Milestone and boundaries

**First useful milestone:** verified FPT FY2025 income, balance and cash-flow fields visible end to end with source/unit/basis disclosure; unsupported fields remain explicit. This is not an FPT-wide, VCI-wide or whole-market acceptance claim. Evidence review and offline repairs can proceed now; production writes, new source acquisition, source-policy changes and deployment require their separate prerequisites and approval. No new widgets or database migration unless the recovery demonstrates a concrete need.
