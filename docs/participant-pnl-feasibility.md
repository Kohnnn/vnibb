# Prediction-market participant P&L leaderboard feasibility

Research for [#73](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/issues/73), 2026-09-28. **Research only; no leaderboard or participant ingestion is implemented.** This question concerns prediction-market *wallet/account performance*, not equity movers, unusual flow, or a ranking of prediction *contracts* by volume, probability change, or resolution.

## Decision

**Conditional, provider-native Polymarket-only board is technically plausible; a complete, cross-provider leaderboard of profitable people is not established.** Polymarket's public Data API v2 actually publishes a ranked wallet board, but its **day/week/month PnL is marked equity change net of flows (includes unrealized changes), while `all` is realized-only lifetime PnL**. It also publishes cumulative PnL components and cashflow data. Displaying those *as provider-defined wallet metrics*, with window/currency/source/freshness and coverage qualifiers, would be more honest than computing a universal participant return from VNIBB's catalogue. This is a feasibility conclusion, **not approval of redistribution rights or a proposal to implement now**. [Polymarket v2 leaderboard](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/boards/get-the-trader-leaderboard), [user PnL series](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/wallet/get-a-users-pnl-series), [data freshness](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/service/get-data-freshness).

An observed read-only smoke request on 2026-09-28 to `GET https://data-api.polymarket.com/v2/leaderboard?time_period=all&sort_by=PNL&limit=1` returned HTTP 200 with `{data:[{rank,user_id,pnl,volume,user_name,profile_image,x_username,verified}],pagination:{limit,offset,has_more,next_cursor}}`; this proves *one public response shape*, **not** complete historical coverage, metric accuracy, or permission to republish. Do not copy a sampled person's financial amount or identity into VNIBB research/fixtures. The [official v2 schema](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/boards/get-the-trader-leaderboard) defines the contract independently of that probe.

### VNIBB as built (observed source code, not provider promises)

- The five source ingesters are Polymarket Gamma, Kalshi, PredictIt, Limitless and Manifold; they populate **market-level** rows, not persons. The normalized table has `(source, source_id)` uniqueness, question/status, aggregate volume/liquidity, outcome labels/prices and metadata; `source_id` is a *market* key, not a user ID. [Model](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/models/prediction_market.py#L22-L56), [orchestration](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/services/populate_prediction_markets.py#L23-L85), [Gamma normalization](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/services/prediction_market_service.py#L278-L327).
- Kalshi's public-market normalization maps aggregate open interest to generic liquidity, **not an individual position**; PredictIt priced contracts collapse into a market-level observation. No provider's participant fills/identity survive this pipeline. [Kalshi mapper](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/services/kalshi_service.py#L138-L175), [PredictIt mapper](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/services/predictit_service.py#L77-L109), [read schemas](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/api/v1/prediction_markets.py#L60-L160).
- Daily/15-minute snapshots observe a **contract's first-outcome quote** plus aggregate volume/liquidity. Their limited retention and bounded fresh eligible catalogue cannot be converted into a participant cost basis, realized payout or complete lifetime ledger. The maintainer lifecycle diagram explicitly describes first-outcome changes, coverage windows, retention and the catalogue→widget path; it is a *market* lifecycle, not a participant accounting map. [Daily model](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/models/prediction_market_snapshot.py#L22-L59), [intraday model](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/apps/api/vnibb/models/prediction_market_intraday_snapshot.py#L29-L69), [widget lifecycle map](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/docs/WIDGET_SYSTEM_REFERENCE.md#L183-L235), [API coverage](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/docs/API_REFERENCE.md#L125-L148).

### Truthful alternative and gates

1. **Immediately viable without participant claims:** keep provider-labelled *market* discovery, price-history/mover and volume views. Label as contract outcomes/odds and explicitly **not trader profitability**; do not rename market movers a P&L leaderboard. [VNIBB lifecycle reference](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/blob/7f969d2/docs/WIDGET_SYSTEM_REFERENCE.md#L183-L235).
2. **Conditional separate research/build candidate:** a *Polymarket-published wallet leaderboard*, not VNIBB-computed or cross-provider `top profitable participants`; use **v2** (v1 retires 2026-10-24), label `all` realized-only lifetime versus finite-window marked changes, USDC PnL versus share-volume, source/time/lag, wallets vs people, and provider methodology. Preserve null/unranked and cursor semantics. Before any implementation: provider distribution/display rights and VNIBB privacy review; verify sample windows, category/combo inclusion, refresh consistency, fees/flows and provider methodology with production evidence. [Migration](https://docs.polymarket.com/migrate/data-api-v1-to-v2), [v2 leaderboard](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/boards/get-the-trader-leaderboard).
3. **Explicit non-goal at present:** a five-provider, dollar-denominated *individual* P&L or ROI leaderboard, a verified-person ranking, and an independently auditable realized+unrealized net return computed from VNIBB's market snapshots. Kalshi's public trades lack identity; authenticated account ledgers do not authorize publishing strangers' performance; Manifold Mana is not USDC. Rights for Polymarket and other providers must be confirmed, not presumed. [Kalshi public trades](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-trades), [Kalshi fills](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-fills), [Manifold terms](https://docs.manifold.markets/terms).

**Open unknowns:** Polymarket's exact ranking inclusion/exclusion thresholds and historical backfill guarantees beyond its published API contract; whether a user may rotate proxy wallets and how wallet-link changes are represented; exact VNIBB republication/attribution/retention license from Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt and Limitless; regional privacy obligations and participant notice/opt-out; other providers' complete public identity-linked accounting contracts. None is silently treated as a positive license or complete ledger. This is not legal advice.
