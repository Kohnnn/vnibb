# Matrix securities-company metric availability and comparability

Research resolution for [issue #54](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/issues/54), supporting the [Matrix specification map #35](https://github.com/Kohnnn/vnibb/issues/35).

- Research date: 2026-09-26.
- Isolated branch recorded before research: `research/matrix-securities-evidence`.
- Pinned source/base: `20fa4c392e22a42b2d150de22989bb2331a98adb` (Kohnnn/vnibb).
- Scope: a later securities-company playbook, not the first non-financial implementation. Anchor plus preselected peers and a shared latest common fiscal year remain the specification context. These are evidence-backed candidate families, not approved columns or scoring formulas.
- Method: pinned repository source review; official Vnstock documentation; Ministry of Finance (MOF) legal database metadata and HTML regulatory replies. No production database, market/AI provider calls, live issuer observations, PDFs or corpus acquisition; no app changes, builds, lint or tests.

## Resolution

VNIBB has generic financial-statement and earnings/book-valuation fields that can represent securities-company observations, but the reviewed normalization is not a securities-specific accounting contract. It does **not** establish comparable brokerage fees, margin loans, proprietary investment/mark-to-market or regulatory capital-safety observations. The existence of a field, alias or raw-data container is not proof of live coverage, original issuer definition, audit status, scale or revision identity.

In particular, `revenue_from_securities_business_01_11` becomes generic `revenue`, `operating_expenses_21_33` becomes `cost_of_revenue`, and `net_cash_flows_from_securities_trading_activities` becomes `operating_cash_flow`. None of those aliases establishes brokerage revenue, cost of brokerage or proprietary investment earnings. A bank-style loans alias becomes `accounts_receivable`; this is not evidence of a margin-loan series. The candidate-specific unknowns below must remain visible rather than being replaced with convenient generic ratios. [C1]

The possible later playbooks are therefore (a) carefully qualified provider-reported earnings/book observations, (b) a business-line/asset-composition playbook when exact brokerage, lending and investment keys are evidenced, and (c) a separate regulatory-safety playbook when original safety reports and their regulatory basis are available. These alternatives do not approve columns, weights, peers, threshold formulas or a first implementation.

## Evidence vocabulary

- **Code-mapped**: an exact accepted key and a transformation exist at the pinned commit. This does not mean the provider currently returns the key for a securities company.
- **Provider-documented**: first-party public documentation describes an API/schema. Examples are not observed production records or assurance of historical coverage.
- **Regulator-confirmed**: an official regulator page actually read supports the specific statement. A circular catalogue entry proves identity/scope/vintage, not the unread contents of every appendix.
- **Unverified**: no live observations were requested. All issuer coverage, missingness frequencies, newest shared fiscal year, restatement histories and issuer/audit provenance remain unverified.

## Original regulatory definitions and vintage boundaries

The following evidence was read from the **official** MOF site, not a secondary legal summary:

1. **Securities accounting is its own regime.** MOF's catalogue describes 210/2014/TT-BTC as accounting guidance for securities companies, issued 2014-12-30 and effective 2016-01-01. It describes 334/2016/TT-BTC, issued/effective 2016-12-27 in that catalogue, as amending and replacing appendices 02 and 04 of Circular 210. The catalogue also lists 23/2018/TT-BTC for covered-warrant accounting, effective 2018-04-27. These entries justify recording accounting vintage rather than treating a renamed provider field as a timeless definition. Full original appendix line codes and all amendment transitions were not verified without attachments. [R1]
2. **Financial safety is distinct from ordinary book leverage.** MOF's catalogue identifies 91/2020/TT-BTC, issued 2020-11-13 and effective 2021-01-01, as financial-safety indicators and treatment of non-compliant securities businesses. This note does not reproduce an unverified capital formula or threshold from memory. Applicable amendments, calculation details and issuer safety-report coverage remain unresolved. [R2]
3. **Proprietary portfolios can contain hedges.** In its 2026-07-09 reply, MOF's Accounting and Auditing Supervisory Department confirms detailed accounts 121/124 for existing securities used for covered-warrant hedging, disclosure of total securities used for hedging under 23/2018, and quotes 122/2025/TT-BTC Article 10(4)(c), requiring independent management/accounting of the hedging portfolio. The question quotes FVTPL sale-cost wording from 334/2016, but the regulator's answer is about hedging accounting; this note does not promote the questioner's numerical example or costing interpretation to regulator approval. [R3]
4. **Entity and date can be legally decisive.** MOF's 2025-04-04 reply on 121/2020 Article 28 says investment/contribution value is compared with equity of the securities company itself **at the same calculation time**. This is evidence against substituting convenient consolidated latest-year equity for every regulatory purpose. It is not a blanket interpretation of every capital-safety denominator or an approval of a Matrix threshold. The catalogue gives Circular 121 an effective date of 2021-02-15. [R4]

The legal database displayed these entries as in force at access time; that display is not an exhaustive legal-consolidation audit. Where original definitions or appendices are absent, retain the absence instead of filling it with bank or non-financial analogues.

## Provider contract and version risk

Vnstock's first-party migration guide says `vnstock_data` 3.2.8 moved financial statements to long-form rows with `id`, `name`, `unit`, `order`, `level`; the Fundamental schema also shows `period`, `off_balance`, `value`. `com_type='Securities'` is documented alongside Regular/Bank/Insurance, and wide/time-series output uses semantic IDs. A full migration-reference CSV is linked behind the sponsor account rather than reproduced publicly. This establishes provider-documented company-type support, **not** a verified securities metric inventory or the version/contract actually used by VNIBB. [P1, P2]

The release history records a VCI annual-period mixing fix in 3.2.7 (2026-08-03), normalization in 3.2.8 (2026-08-16), a regression/reversion in 3.2.9, and restoration of `com_type`, `drop_empty`, `format` and default Vietnamese language in 3.3.0 (2026-09-15). Hence package/source/schema revision is part of observation identity, not an implementation detail that can be ignored when comparing years or peers. No installation or market call was made to determine deployed version or compatibility. [P3]

## Ingestion, serving and preservation limits

### Two different acquisition paths

The runtime statement fetcher considers VCI, configured source and KBS, scores payloads, then may supplement selected fields from other candidates. Its key normalizer and common map are not a securities-specific chart of accounts. Both gross-looking and net/parent-attributable labels can share a normalized destination. Only tax and SG&A are additive pivot targets; mapping several labels to `revenue` does **not** mean the code sums all those business lines. [C1]

The separate Mongo catalog backfill calls `Fundamental().equity(symbol)` financial methods and writes `dataset` such as `finance.income_statement`, `datasetVariant` such as `finance.income_statement.year`, `source='vnstock-data'`, `providerSource='vnstock_data'`, `recordKey`, `observedAt`, and `raw`. This records an acquisition wrapper, not an issuer-original publication identifier or proof that the upstream vendor was VCI. The `raw` row is the library output, not a signed financial statement. [C4]

### Units, periods and revisions

- The statement pivot path multiplies KBS numeric mapped metrics by 1,000 except EPS/diluted EPS. The non-pivot transform has no equivalent blanket scale operation. Names containing `bn_vnd` also occur in accepted aliases. Therefore a universal assertion that every normalized amount is verified VND is unsafe; preserve original unit/scale and the transform/version that produced it. [C1]
- Statement normalization supports fiscal-year labels and quarter labels; the service can inject YTD and calculate TTM. Annual and quarterly Mongo variants share the dataset name. A request for `year`, a latest-row tool, or a visible year label alone is not proof that all peers are using a common complete annual observation. [C3, C4]
- Income/cashflow are flows; balance-sheet values are stocks. TTM/YTD and a latest-quarter balance do not silently replace the chosen common fiscal year. Quarter-only versus cumulative interim basis also needs original evidence before aggregation. [C3]
- The standardized model has `raw_data` and `updated_at`, but the pivot path constructs output without `raw_data`; only the non-pivot path explicitly retains `raw_data=row`. Do not promise original raw keys survive every path. [C1]
- ORM uniqueness is `(symbol, period, period_type)`, and financial ratios also carry fiscal year/quarter and generic source/raw data. No per-metric immutable disclosure revision, audit authority or original source URL is established by these columns. `updated_at`/`observedAt` is not the issuer's publication time. A restatement cannot be identified merely because the fiscal year and value differ. [C4, C5]

### API, VniAgent and external MCP

The statement API delegates to the financial service; the ratio API merges provider/Mongo/Postgres data and can create null-valued period rows from statement support. Code presence in these serving paths does not establish a populated securities dataset. Generic ratio enrichment has a bank-only classification guard, whereas fundamental valuation uses financial-industry terms including securities to select RIM. Thus generic derived ratios should not be accepted as securities-native observations merely because another subsystem classifies the same issuer as financial. This is a code-level applicability risk, not a claim that an invalid value was observed live. [C3, C6, C7]

VniAgent's context service takes a latest FinancialRatio row and emits generic valuation, profitability and leverage fields. External MCP separately exposes `financial_ratios`, latest-statement/latest-ratio tools, and premium `finance.*` datasets. A dataset allowlist entry does not identify an annual `datasetVariant`; neither consumer path establishes this research's exact common-year, unit, definition or frozen-source requirements by itself. Both are required future consumers, but no Matrix contract is implemented or approved in this research. [C8, C9]

For any later securities Matrix observation, evidence eligibility requires retaining or explicitly marking unknown: issuer/entity and company type; original metric key and label; normalized destination and transformation/version; period type/start/end and fiscal year; currency/scale; consolidation; gross/net and parent/minority attribution; provider/source and retrieval time; original disclosure/publication/revision identity and audit status; valuation price date/share basis where relevant. These are evidence constraints, not a new storage design or user column selection.

## Remaining gaps and bounded alternatives

| Gap | Consequence for later specification |
| --- | --- |
| No live per-peer coverage/common-year check | Do not claim any issuer has a populated row or choose a concrete latest common year from this note. |
| Exact securities provider taxonomy and original appendix line mappings unavailable publicly in reviewed pages | A detailed brokerage/lending/investment candidate stays unverified even if a generic alias has a number. |
| Margin-only balance and income split, funding composition, gross/net allowance basis absent | No invented margin utilization, loan yield, funding spread, leverage-safety equivalence or segment profitability formula. |
| Realized/unrealized investment results, portfolio category, hedge segregation absent | No proprietary earnings-quality or mark-to-market decomposition from revenue/OCF proxies. |
| Regulatory numerator/denominator and current-vintage details absent | A separate safety playbook cannot use ordinary equity/debt ratios as a substitute or assert compliance. |
| Per-metric consolidation/audit/revision authority absent | Report unknown, not “audited”, “issuer-filed”, “restated” or “consolidated” by default. |
| Units and package-interface changes | Annual comparability requires original scale and version evidence, not a magnitude heuristic alone. |
| Rights not established | Public documentation, API accessibility, sponsor entitlement and open-source code do not prove storage, redistribution, commercial display or external-MCP rights for underlying market data. No legal grant was verified. |

The research issue is resolved by this bounded evidence inventory and explicit ineligibility/unknowns. It does not resolve human column decisions, authorize provider acquisition, assert live coverage, or move the securities playbook into the first implementation.
