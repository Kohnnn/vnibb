## Dependencies and boundaries
S1 establishes the baseline; S2 and S3 are independent investigations. S4 and S5 can proceed independently after S1 and must preserve S3 admission contracts. S6 requires confirmed provider ownership before acquisition; S7 can progress independently but cannot be simulated into a production PASS. Final verification follows all implemented slices. Existing QA tickets remain the tracker; do not recreate already repaired #115–#123 defects solely because issues are open.

No paid providers, new framework, broad redesign, guessed units, blanket retries, automatic historical-price cleanup, corpus replacement, auth-policy changes or deployment in this planning pass. The cheaper alternative is S1 plus the confirmed wording fix first; preserve evidence-blocked data gaps rather than invent repairs.
